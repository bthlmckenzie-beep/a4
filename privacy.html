Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Official Governance of LayerDistrict Atelier

1. Commitment to Visitor Privacy

At LayerDistrict ("we," "our," or "us"), located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our readership, researchers, and clients. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, protect, and handle data submitted via our website.

2. Information Collection Protocols

We collect personal information solely when submitted voluntarily through our climate consultation forms, newsletter subscriptions, or direct correspondence. This may include:

  • Full Name and Professional Affiliation
  • Contact information including email address and telephone number
  • Expedition parameters, climate queries, and garment specifications
  • Anonymized technical browsing telemetry (browser version, operating system, device classification)

3. Usage of Personal Data

Your information is utilized strictly to fulfill technical inquiries, provide requested outerwear research, and optimize website functionality. We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal data to third-party marketing entities under any circumstances.

4. Data Security Standards

We maintain strict SSL encryption, secured server environments, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

5. Rights Under GDPR and CCPA

Visitors from the European Union or California hold statutory rights to access, rectify, or request deletion of their personal records. To exercise these rights, contact our privacy director at privacy@layerdistrict.com or call +1-888-777-5845.

6. Bureau Contact Coordinates

For inquiries regarding our privacy standards, write to LayerDistrict Bureau, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
