Cookie Preferences

Last updated: January 2026 • Official Governance of LayerDistrict Atelier

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser to record session states, remember interface preferences, and provide aggregated analytics.

2. Cookie Usage on LayerDistrict

LayerDistrict employs cookies exclusively for essential site functionality and performance:

  • Preference Cookies: Preserving your selected theme (Glacier Night vs. Alpine Snow Light) across browsing sessions.
  • Performance Analytics: Collecting anonymized visitor telemetry via Google Analytics to evaluate which technical articles and membrane comparisons generate the greatest readership interest.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You may configure your browser to decline cookies or alert you when a cookie is placed. Note that disabling cookies may reset your theme preferences upon page reload.
